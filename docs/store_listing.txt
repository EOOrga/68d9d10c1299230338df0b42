App name
SCP Operations

Short description
Plan resources, assets, maintenance, and containers in one local board.

Full description
SCP Operations helps daily business teams coordinate resource allocation, asset readiness, maintenance requests, and container tracking from a focused Android workspace.

Create local records, assign owners, follow status changes, and keep the next handover clear without account registration or cloud sync. The app is designed for practical operational follow-up on the device you use each day.
